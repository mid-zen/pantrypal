How this works (30-second version)

Different betting apps price the same game differently. Occasionally the best price on each side — taken from two different apps — adds up to under 100%. When that happens, you split your money across both sides and win no matter who wins.

The Edge % is your guaranteed return. We only show books that are legal in Ontario. Lines move fast, so always re-check the price in the app before you bet.

Demo data shows two built-in example games (the same ones every scan) so you can see what an arb looks like without an API key. Live odds scans current real prices — it needs a free key from the-odds-api.com in arb-scanner/.env on the server.
